The model

Made by
whoever's there

Open CrowdControl and your GPS puts you in up to five community stations — your neighbourhood, city, state, country, and the whole world. You're in all of them simultaneously.

When the station needs a new track, the server picks a random listener, then a random song from their active Spotify playlists. No stored queue. No trained taste graph. The pool is whoever's tuned in right now.

Add your playlists, tune in, and your music immediately enters the mix for everyone at your level. You contribute; the crowd contributes back.

What you can do

Listen.
React. Shape it.

🎵
Crowd-sourced pool

The track pool is the live Spotify libraries of whoever is tuned in. Your taste changes the station the moment you join.

⏱
Real-time sync

Tune in and Spotify starts at the exact position everyone else is at. Mid-song, in sync, from the first second.

⏭
Skip voting

When enough listeners request a skip, the station advances. Collective control — no single veto, no autoplay tyranny.

⚡
Fast Pass Premium

Queue a specific track from the station's pool to play next. One slot per listener, played before the random algorithm runs.

♥
App Favorites

Heart a track to save it to your App Favorites playlist — separate from Spotify Liked Songs and fully editable.

📡
TV & Apple Vision

Runs on Apple TV, Android TV, and visionOS. Pick your city manually and enjoy the crowd from the couch.

For creators

Your
station.

  • Static venue or dynamic GPS — the station follows you
  • Sound filters: BPM range, genre whitelist, release year, explicit flag
  • Advanced query builder with and/or logic across any audio feature
  • DJ Mode — next track scored for harmonic continuity and drawn from the top quartile
  • Track pool weights — prioritise your own music or recent arrivals
  • Configurable skip threshold and Fast Pass toggle
  • Password-protect or go public within your broadcast radius
