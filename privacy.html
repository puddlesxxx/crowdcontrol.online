Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy.
No surprises.

Effective: August 2026 · CrowdControl

CrowdControl is a radio app. We need to know roughly where you are and what music you have. This document explains exactly what we collect, why, how it's stored, and what you can do about it. We've written it in plain English on purpose.

Overview

What we collect

Location An H3 hexagonal cell index at resolution 7 (~1 km² area). Never precise GPS coordinates.
Spotify account Your Spotify user ID, display name, and the tracks in playlists you choose to activate. No payment data, no private messages.
Play history Tracks played per station, stored for 7 days. Used to power the History tab and prevent repeats within 6 hours.
Reactions Like, dislike, and skip-request counts per track per station. No individual attribution beyond the station level.
Subscription status Whether your account has an active Premium subscription, via RevenueCat. No payment card details are ever stored by CrowdControl.
Session token A signed JSON Web Token (JWT) issued by our server and stored locally on your device. Used to authenticate API requests.
What we never collect: Precise GPS coordinates, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, browsing history outside the app, or any data from third-party advertising networks.

Location

How location works

Your device computes an H3 hexagonal cell index (resolution 7, roughly 1 km²) from your GPS coordinates before anything leaves your device. Only the cell ID is sent to our server — never the raw latitude/longitude.

We use that cell to look up your city, state, and country via a geocoding reverse-lookup cached in Cloudflare KV. The cell is stored in our database linked to your user ID. The city, state, and country names that result from the lookup are also stored so we can show you the correct station labels.

Background location

If you grant background location permission, CrowdControl can update your station when you move to a new area while the app is minimised. This is entirely optional — you can revoke background permission in your device settings at any time. We never record a continuous GPS trail; only cell transitions matter to us.

Manual location (TV & visionOS)

On Apple TV, Android TV, and visionOS, there is no GPS hardware. You choose your city from a list or type a search term. That city is stored as your location with no coordinates attached.

Geofences (for private station radius checks) are computed entirely on-device using iOS/Android system APIs. The geofence boundaries are downloaded from our server — your precise position relative to them never leaves your device.

Spotify

Spotify data

CrowdControl uses the Spotify OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow. You authorise CrowdControl directly with Spotify; we receive a short-lived access token (60-minute expiry) and a refresh token. We never see your Spotify password.

The scopes we request are limited to what we need:

streaming Play audio via the Spotify SDK on your device.
user-read-playback-state Know the current playback position so we can sync listeners to the right offset.
user-modify-playback-state Add tracks to your queue and control playback for tune-in and track advancement.
playlist-read-private Read your private playlists so you can choose which to activate for the radio pool.
user-library-read Mirror your Spotify Liked Songs into the Liked Songs playlist in the app (read-only).
user-read-private
user-read-email Read your Spotify display name and user ID to create your CrowdControl account. Your email is used for account identification only — it is not shared or used for marketing.

Track metadata (title, artist, album art, duration) from your playlists is stored in our Cloudflare D1 database and used to populate the radio pool. We do not sell, license, or share this data. Audio feature enrichment (BPM, key, energy) is fetched from the ReccoBeats API.

Third parties

Who else is involved

Spotify AB OAuth authentication and music playback. Governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.
Cloudflare, Inc. Our backend runs entirely on Cloudflare Workers, D1 (SQLite), and KV. Data is processed at Cloudflare edge locations. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
RevenueCat Subscription management and purchase verification. RevenueCat receives anonymised purchase receipts from Apple/Google. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
ReccoBeats Audio feature enrichment (BPM, key, energy, danceability). Track Spotify IDs are sent to their API; no user identifiers are included.
Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) Reverse-geocoding H3 cell centroids to city/state/country names on web and TV platforms. Only the H3 centroid coordinates are sent, not your precise location. Results are cached for 90 days in Cloudflare KV.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking pixels. Crash reporting, if added, will be disclosed here first.

Infrastructure

Where your data lives

Cloudflare D1 Primary database: user accounts, playlist tracks, play history, private station settings, credits, follower graph. SQLite. Stored in Cloudflare's global network.
Cloudflare KV Short-lived cache: now-playing seeds, listener presence counts, skip vote tallies, fast pass queues, geocoding results. Keys expire automatically.
Cloudflare Durable Objects Per-station in-memory WebSocket hubs. Listener presence is buffered in memory and flushed periodically — no persistent personal data stored here.
Device (SecureStore / localStorage) Your JWT, Spotify access/refresh tokens, and most-recently-known location are stored locally on your device in the OS secure store. They never leave your device except as Bearer tokens on API requests over HTTPS.

Defensive design

Security measures

We treat security as a design constraint, not an afterthought. Below are the specific measures in place:

Authentication & tokens

PKCE OAuth — no secret in the client HS256 JWT, 7-day TTL Refresh flow with short-lived Spotify AT (60 min) JWT stored in SecureStore / localStorage — not cookies

Transport

All traffic over HTTPS / WSS TLS enforced at Cloudflare edge HSTS headers

Location privacy

Coordinates quantised to H3 res-7 on device Raw GPS never transmitted No continuous location trail stored

API design

All mutation endpoints require a valid JWT Skip votes deduplicated server-side per userId Fast pass limited to one entry per user Private station access gated by ownership check Demo auth endpoint absent in production

Data minimisation

We store only what the app needs to function. Playlist tracks contain no listening history beyond which station they played in. Individual reaction data (like/dislike) is aggregated per track; it is not exposed to other listeners in its raw form.

No ads, no profiles, no tracking. We have no advertising relationships and run no behaviour-profiling analytics. Your Spotify usage outside of CrowdControl is never visible to us.

Data lifecycle

How long we keep things

Play history 7 days. Rows are automatically expired by a expires_at column checked on every query.
Skip votes 5 minutes. Stored in Cloudflare KV with a TTL keyed to the track.
Fast pass queue entries Up to 1 hour, or until the track is played or manually removed.
Geocoding cache H3 cell → city lookups cached for 90 days.
Playlist tracks Retained for as long as the playlist remains active in your account. Deleting or deactivating a playlist removes its tracks from the pool.
User account Retained until you request deletion. We do not automatically delete accounts for inactivity.

Your rights

What you can do

Delete your data

You can clear all locally stored credentials and cached app data at any time using Profile → Privacy → Reset to Defaults. This removes your JWT, cached location, and settings from the device. To delete your account and all associated server-side data, contact us at the address below.

Revoke Spotify access

You can disconnect your Spotify account from Profile → Disconnect Spotify. You can also revoke CrowdControl's access directly from your Spotify account settings at any time. Revoking access stops playlist sync and playback control; it does not automatically delete your CrowdControl account or play history.

Disable background location

Background location can be toggled off in Profile → Privacy → Background Location Updates, or by revoking the permission from your device's system settings (Settings → CrowdControl → Location → Never / While Using).

Data portability

On request, we can provide a JSON export of your account data: user record, active playlists and tracks, and play history within the retention window. Contact us to request this.

GDPR & CCPA

If you are located in the EU/EEA or California, you have additional rights including the right to access, correct, port, and erase your data. Our legal basis for processing is contractual necessity (running the service you signed up for) and, for location data, your explicit consent via the system permission dialog. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
